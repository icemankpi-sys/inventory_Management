Item master

CSV columns: ItemCode, Description, UnitBarcode, CartonBarcode, UnitsPerCarton, C40UOM (UNIT or CARTON). An item with more than one barcode: separate them with | (e.g. 4791234567890|4799876543210). Item codes and descriptions come from the C40 stock export; barcodes are read from the packs once.

Cloud database

Send the item master loaded on this device to the database, so every phone gets the same items and barcodes after sign-in.

Users and access

Admin: everything, including users. Count lead: stock takes, plans, counting. Counter: counting only. To change a password, add the same username again.
